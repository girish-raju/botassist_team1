Botree Support Operations — Ticket Handling Guidelines

The Botree support desk handles three categories of tickets: Distribution Management System (DMS) issues, Secondary Sales reporting queries, and Retailer onboarding requests. DMS issues are classified as P1 (system down, SLA: 2 hours), P2 (feature degraded, SLA: 8 hours), or P3 (cosmetic or minor, SLA: 48 hours). All tickets are routed through the central helpdesk and assigned to the appropriate regional support pod based on the distributor's territory mapping. Escalation follows the standard chain: L1 Support Agent, L2 Technical Specialist, L3 Engineering, with automatic escalation triggers if SLA thresholds are breached by more than 25 percent.

The support team operates in three shifts covering IST business hours (8 AM to 10 PM) with a skeleton crew for after-hours P1 incidents. Each pod consists of two L1 agents, one L2 specialist, and a shared L3 engineer across pods. Monthly reviews track First Response Time (target: under 15 minutes for P1), Resolution Rate (target: 85 percent within SLA), and Customer Satisfaction Score (target: 4.2 out of 5). The Knowledge Base is updated weekly by L2 specialists with new resolution patterns, and BotAssist is being deployed to surface these KB articles automatically when agents handle incoming tickets, reducing average handle time by an estimated 30 percent.
